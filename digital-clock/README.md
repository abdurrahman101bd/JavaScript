## ✨ Features

- **Real-time clock** — updates every second, perfectly synced with your device
- **12-hour / 24-hour toggle** — switch formats with a single click
- **Live date & day** — shows current date and weekday automatically
- **Live weather** — powered by [Open-Meteo](https://open-meteo.com/) (100% free, no API key needed)
- **Dynamic greetings** — changes based on time of day:
  - Good morning (5 AM – 12 PM) — golden glow
  - Good afternoon (12 PM – 5 PM) — sky blue glow
  - Good evening (5 PM – 9 PM) — orange glow
  - Good night (9 PM – 5 AM) — purple glow
- **Theme-adaptive colors** — clock digits change color to match the current greeting theme
- **Neon glow effects** — soft, eye-friendly text-shadow on clock digits
- **Blinking colon** — classic clock behavior between hours, minutes, and seconds
- **Fully responsive** — works perfectly on mobile (320px+), tablet, and desktop
- **Smooth animations** — animated gradient border, floating glow orbs, and fade transitions
- **Dark mode only** — designed for sleek, modern aesthetics

---

> ⚠️ **Note:** Weather feature needs `HTTPS` or `localhost` to access the Geolocation API.
